Central Processing Unit (CPU Architecture)

📖 1. Definition & Real-World Analogy

Definition: The Central Processing Unit (CPU) is the primary component of a computer that interprets instructions and processes data contained within computer programs.

Real-World Analogy: Think of the CPU like the Human Brain in a human body. Just as the brain receives signals from eyes/ears, processes thoughts, and commands hands/legs what to do, the CPU processes inputs from mouse/keyboard and executes tasks on screen.
