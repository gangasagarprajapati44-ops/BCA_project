Central Processing Unit (CPU Architecture)

📖 1. Definition & Analogy

Definition: The Central Processing Unit (CPU) is the primary component of a computer that interprets instructions and processes data.

Real-World Analogy: Think of the CPU like the human brain taking decisions based on inputs received from sense organs.

❓ 3. Short & Long Q&A

Q1: What are the main internal components of a CPU?
Answer: The main components are the Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU), Control Unit (CU), and Registers cache memory.
