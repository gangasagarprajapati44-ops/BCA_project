❓ 3. Short & Long Q&A

Q1: What are the main internal components of a CPU?
Answer: The main components are the Arithmetic Logic Unit (ALU), Control Unit (CU), and Registers cache memory.
Q2: Explain the working cycle of a processor in brief.
Answer: Processor follows the Instruction Cycle: Fetch, Decode, Execute, and Writeback.
